Privacy Policy

Applies to Wearcade. Last updated 21 August 2026.

Wearcade collects no data at all. It has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no network connection. Nothing you do in the app leaves your watch.

What the app stores

Wearcade saves your high scores and your sound and music preferences on the device itself, using the operating system's standard local storage. This information is never transmitted anywhere, is not readable by us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

Wearcade does not collect personal information, does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. It does not request access to your location, health data, contacts, microphone, or camera.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Purchases

Wearcade is a paid app on the App Store and a free download with a single unlock purchase on Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Apple and Google — we never see or receive your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date.
