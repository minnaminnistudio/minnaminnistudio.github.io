Frequently asked questions

Does Wearcade need an iPhone?
No — it's a watch-only app. Nothing to install on your phone.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. Every game is included at one price, forever.

Does it work offline?
Yes — no internet connection is required to play.

Where are my high scores kept?
On your watch, and nowhere else. They are not synced or uploaded, so deleting the app clears them.

There's no sound.
Check that your watch isn't in Silent Mode, and that sound is enabled in Wearcade's own Settings screen.

The screen dims while I'm playing.
watchOS controls the screen timeout and an app can't hold it open. Raising your wrist or tapping the screen wakes it again.
